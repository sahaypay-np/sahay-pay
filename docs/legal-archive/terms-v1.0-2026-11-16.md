# Sahay Pay — Terms of Service (v1.0)

*Version 1.0 — Last updated 16 November 2026. Effective 16 November 2026. The English version of these terms is the binding version.*

## 1. What Sahay Pay is

Sahay Pay connects people in Nepal and India who need help with a bill with people willing to help. A supporter pays the person directly, from their own payment app, using the payout details shown in the app. Sahay Pay never holds, receives, processes, or guarantees gift money.

Payments are currently in Demo Mode. The gift journey is a demonstration: fees shown are quotes and are not collected, and no real money moves through Sahay Pay. Any transfer you make is made by you, from your own payment app, directly to the other person.

## 2. Who can use Sahay Pay

Sahay Pay is for adults aged 18 and over only. Anyone under 18 is blocked and no account is created. You must give true information about yourself, and you may only create one account.

## 3. Your account

Your account is created from simple questions (name, age, country, city, about you). There is no email address or password. When your account is created you are shown a one-time recovery code: write it down and keep it somewhere safe — it is the only way back into your account if you log out, clear your browser data, or change device, because Sahay Pay never stores the code itself. Your login stays on your device for 30 days. Keep your device secure, and log out on any device you share. You can delete your account in Settings at any time.

## 4. Posting a bill

Post only real bills, described honestly. Every post is reviewed by the owner before it goes public, and the owner may approve it, reject it, or ask for changes. A material edit to a live bill sends it back to review. Keep other people personal details out of your photos. A bill may be paused if it is reported, and removed if it breaks these terms.

## 5. Verification

Bill, ID, and Name badges are decided by a human reviewer. A badge means the reviewer checked what was shown to them. It is not a promise that a bill is genuine or that a person will use help in a particular way.

## 6. Helping and gifts

Gifts are voluntary. A bill counts as helped only when the person confirms they received the money. No contract of sale is formed through Sahay Pay: a gift is a voluntary transfer between two users, and it counts only when the recipient confirms receipt. Sahay Pay cannot reverse a gift and cannot refund you, because the money never passes through Sahay Pay. Never send money you cannot afford to lose. Never share your MPIN, OTP, password, or card details with anyone. Sahay Pay never asks for them.

If a gift goes wrong, the dispute is between the two users involved. You can report the problem through the Help Centre complaint form and Sahay Pay will look into it and may suspend the account involved, but Sahay Pay cannot reverse a transfer it never held and cannot force a refund.

## 7. Fees

The fee shown at the Fee step is the current schedule: in Nepal, Rs 160 on gifts up to Rs 1,600, then 10% of the gift; in India, Rs 100 on gifts up to Rs 1,000, then 10% of the gift. Charity asks are free. While Demo Mode is active, fees are quoted only and are not collected. The fee may change, and the fee shown before you confirm always applies.

## 8. Cross-border help

If you help someone across a border, follow your own country law. Indian law generally restricts foreign citizens from donating to individuals in India without registration. Transfers between UPI and Nepal have limits (India to Nepal up to INR 200,000 per transaction; Nepal to India up to INR 15,000 per transaction). This is general information only, not legal advice.

## 9. Brands and auctions

Brand asks and auctions are arrangements between users and brands. Sahay Pay does not guarantee any brand deal, introduction, or auction outcome.

## 10. What is not allowed

Fake bills, fraud, impersonation, illegal content, harassment, spam, scraping, and attempts to break the service are not allowed. Reported illegal content is removed promptly. Accounts that break these rules may be suspended.

If you believe something posted on Sahay Pay infringes your copyright, report it through the Help Centre complaint form. Include: what was copied and where it appears on Sahay Pay, proof that you own it (or act for the owner), and how to reach you. Sahay Pay will review the report and remove content that breaks the rules.
