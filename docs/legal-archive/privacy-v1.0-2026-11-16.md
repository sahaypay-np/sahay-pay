# Sahay Pay — Privacy Policy (v1.0)

*Version 1.0 — Last updated 16 November 2026. Effective 16 November 2026. The English version of this policy is the binding version.*

## 1. What we collect

Profile: display name, age, country, city, about you, language, and an optional photo. Payout details: your eSewa or Khalti number, UPI ID, or bank details, encrypted and shown publicly only as a masked hint. Bills you post: amount, category, description, bill photo, and payment method. Verification documents, which only the reviewer sees and which are deleted after review. Reports and complaints you send. Session cookies that keep you logged in (see §8 for the list). A scrambled copy (hash) of your one-time recovery code, so we can check a code you enter without ever storing the code itself.

We do not collect email addresses, passwords, OTPs, MPINs, card numbers, or banking credentials.

## 2. How we use it

To run the service and show your bill to helpers; to review posts and keep the service safe; to show your payout details to a helper only at the Send step; and to answer complaints. We do not sell your data. We do not run third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

## 3. Who sees it

Your public profile and bill are visible to other users. Your full payout details are shown only to a helper at the Send step, and every reveal is logged. Verification documents are seen only by the reviewer. Our hosting providers, Netlify for the website and Supabase for the database in Mumbai, process data only to run the service.

## 4. How it is protected

Payout details are encrypted while stored. The admin console is protected by a PIN. Access is limited to what the service needs. No system is completely secure, and we cannot promise absolute security.

## 5. How long we keep it

We keep each kind of data only as long as it is needed:

- Your profile and bills: until you delete them.
- Verification documents: deleted after the review is decided.
- Session records: 30 days for your login, 24 hours for the admin login.
- Audit log entries (what happened and when — never your payout details): 1 year.

When you delete a bill or your account, it is removed, including its payout details.

## 6. Your rights

Edit your profile in Settings. Delete your account in Settings at any time. Ask about your data, or request a copy or deletion, through the Help Centre complaint form. We answer access and export requests within 30 days. We respect your rights under the Individual Privacy Act 2075 of Nepal and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 of India.

## 7. Children

Sahay Pay is for adults aged 18 and over only. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. Under-18 sign-ups are blocked and no account is created.

## 8. Cookies

We use two strictly necessary session cookies and no advertising or tracking cookies. Both are HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax, and are marked Secure whenever the site runs in production mode.

| Cookie | Purpose | Lasts |
|---|---|---|
| sp_session | Keeps you logged in | 30 days (renewed when you use the app) |
| sp_admin | Keeps the admin console logged in | 24 hours |

## 9. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the version shown in the app applies. Contact us through the Help Centre complaint form.
