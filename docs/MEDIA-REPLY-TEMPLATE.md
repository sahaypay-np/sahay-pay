# Sahay Pay — Media Reply Template

Phase 2 item 959. For answering press enquiries that arrive through the
complaint form (category "press"). Reply within 2 working days. Keep
answers short, factual, and in plain words. Never share user data, and
never comment on individual users, bills, or complaints.

---

**Subject:** Re: [their subject]

Dear [name],

Thank you for your interest in Sahay Pay. [Answer their specific
question in one or two sentences.]

A few facts that may help:

- Sahay Pay connects people in Nepal and India who need help with a
  bill to people willing to help. Supporters pay the person directly,
  from their own payment app.
- Sahay Pay never holds, receives, processes, or guarantees gift money.
- Every post is reviewed by a human before it goes public.
- The service is currently in Demo Mode: no real payment integration
  is connected, and fees shown are quotes, not charges.
- Sahay Pay is a small, independent service based in Bhairahawa,
  Nepal. It is not a charity, a bank, or a lender.

[If they asked for numbers: point them to the in-app fact sheet —
"Live numbers are on the fact sheet in the app's Help Centre."]

[If they asked for an interview: offer a written Q&A or a short call,
and agree the time by reply.]

[If they asked about a specific user, bill, or complaint: "We don't
comment on individual users or cases, to protect their privacy."]

Happy to help further — just reply here.

Best regards,
[Name], Sahay Pay

---

## Lines to avoid

- Never promise a launch date, a feature, or a number you cannot verify.
- Never say "secure", "safe", or "guaranteed" about user money — Sahay
  Pay never holds it, and no system is completely secure.
- Never share anything from the admin console, the audit log, or a
  complaint.
- Never speculate about a user's motives or a bill's truth — say what
  the review process is instead.
